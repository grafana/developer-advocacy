Speakers included Marty, Zach, and Mario, who discussed the features of the Grafana Tempo 3.0 release. Key topics included the introduction of a single binary deployment mode, improvements in performance and durability, and the integration of Kafka for distributed mode. They emphasized that the single binary mode is ideal for smaller workloads and prototyping, while the distributed mode is suited for production environments requiring high availability and scalability.
