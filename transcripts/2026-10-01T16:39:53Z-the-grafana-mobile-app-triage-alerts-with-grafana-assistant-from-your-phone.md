## Summary

The show is titled "The Grafana Mobile App: Triage Alerts with Grafana Assistant from Your Phone," published on October 1, 2026. Speakers Ignacio and Florian, both from the Grafana mobile team, discussed the features of the Grafana mobile app that allow users to triage alerts and manage incidents directly from their phones. Key features highlighted include the ability to acknowledge alerts, consult the Grafana Assistant for related alerts and next steps, and declare incidents while maintaining context for further investigation. The app aims to streamline the incident response process, enabling users to gather information before returning to their laptops for resolution.

Topics: Grafana mobile app, triaging alerts, Grafana Assistant, incident management, mobile features.

Hi, I am Ignacio. The Grafana mobile app lets you triage alerts with the help of Assistant from your phone. You can even declare an incident or trigger an investigation for that incident if you decide that you need to get more information. This way, by the time you get to your laptop, everything's ready for you. 

We have all been there, right? 3 AM, an alarm goes off, and you go to your laptop to figure out what's going on. You want to know more about that before you reach your laptop. Wouldn't it be great if you had the opportunity to do that while you have your phone? You can trigger an investigation, talk to the Assistant, and figure out what's going on so that by the time you get to your laptop, you already have all the investigation and data you need to resolve the incident.

To demonstrate this, I'm going to hand it over to Florian from the mobile team.

Thanks, Ignacio. So let's imagine it's 2:00 AM in the morning. You're the engineer on call, and you've just been woken up by a series of alerts going off on your phone. Your mind is miles away from work, and you need to quickly understand what's going on. 

You start off by tapping the alert as you normally would. You are sent directly into the app. You acknowledge the alert, and what you can do now is tap the Assistant button. It will send you over to Assistant with the alert already in context, and you can ask it questions about it. For example, you might ask: "Can you triage this? What are the other alerts related? What are possible next steps?"

While Assistant is looking into that, we can quickly check the dashboard. The problem was a latency issue, and the alert was raised due to latency in the payment path. So we can check the payment service dashboard to see if there's any indication of it. Here, we do see that the latency increased quite a bit within the last few minutes, so there seems to be something wrong. 

We can look at the dashboard more closely, zoom in on the timeframe, and we can also add it to our home screen so that if the issue is resolved later, you can easily track it on the board. 

Okay, let's look over to Assistant again and see where it's at. It came back now, and it looks like they are all related and it seems serious. This might be the point where we want to raise an incident. You can do that right from the app. You can go back to your alert group and declare an incident. It carries over all the information from the alert group, so you don't have to do it manually. 

Now, you can tap the Assistant button again. It brings you into Assistant with the incident already in context, and you can ask it to start an investigation. 

With that done, it's probably now time to grab a coffee and continue on your laptop. On your laptop, you can now continue right where you left off on your phone. For IRM incidents, you can see the incident we previously created on the phone, update its status here, and pull in more people if needed. 

The same goes for Assistant. We can look into our earlier investigation, examine the root cause that Assistant turned up, and start to fix the problem. 

So this is the Grafana mobile app with the power of IRM Assistant and dashboards. Download it today from the iOS App Store and the Google Play Store.
