# Single-stack observability at enterprise scale — Bayer on Grafana Cloud

Published on 2026-11-18T16:10:23Z

## Description

When Bayer's IT reorganization handed its team responsibility for thousands of applications — with no unified visibility — the company turned to Grafana Cloud. In this ObservabilityCON on the Road session, Dirk and Christian from Bayer share how they deployed Grafana Cloud in a bring-your-own-cloud configuration, onboarded nearly 4,500 users to a self-service model, and surfaced root causes in a 3-day incident that multiple service providers couldn't solve. Now they're building toward agentic, automated remediation.

## Summary

The community call titled "Single-stack observability at enterprise scale — Bayer on Grafana Cloud" took place on November 18, 2026, featuring speakers Dirk Baumert and Christian Billig from Bayer. They discussed Bayer's transition to Grafana Cloud in a bring-your-own-cloud configuration, which enabled them to manage thousands of applications and onboard nearly 4,500 users to a self-service observability model. Key takeaways included the importance of end-to-end observability, the integration of Grafana tools like Grafana Assistant and Alloy, and the shift towards automated remediation processes. They emphasized the need for a single-stack approach to avoid siloed data and enhance correlation across applications.

Topics: Grafana Cloud, observability, automated remediation, Grafana Assistant, Alloy, single-stack approach.
