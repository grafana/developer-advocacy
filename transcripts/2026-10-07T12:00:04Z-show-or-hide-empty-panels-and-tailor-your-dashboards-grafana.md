## Summary

In the Grafana community call titled "Show or hide empty panels and tailor your dashboards," published on October 7, 2026, the speaker discusses the new feature of show/hide rules for dynamic dashboards. This feature allows dashboard authors to configure panels to be hidden when there is no data, enhancing the user experience by reducing confusion caused by empty panels. The speaker emphasizes that this capability is a significant improvement for users who rely on dashboards for data investigation and learning. Key takeaways include the importance of presenting relevant data upfront and improving the overall usability of dashboards.

Topics: dynamic dashboards, show/hide rules, user experience, data visualization, dashboard customization.
