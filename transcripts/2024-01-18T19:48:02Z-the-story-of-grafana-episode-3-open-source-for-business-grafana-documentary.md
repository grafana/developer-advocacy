## Description

In 2014, Grafana Labs (formerly known as Raintank) was founded with one mission in mind: To build a sustainable business around the popular open source Grafana project and use the revenue from our commercial offerings to re-invest in the technology.

## Summary

The show is titled "The Story of Grafana | Episode 3: Open (Source) for Business," published on January 18, 2024. The speakers include Raj Dutt (Co-founder and CEO), Torkel Ödegaard (Creator of Grafana, Co-founder and Chief Grafana Officer), Daniel Lee (Former Director of Engineering), and several other engineers and developers from Grafana Labs. The episode discusses the origins of Grafana Labs, the transition from a hobby project to a commercial entity, the development of the plugin system, and the company's commitment to open source and community engagement. Key takeaways include the importance of the open SaaS model, the challenges of monetization, and the significance of maintaining a "big tent" approach in the observability space.

Topics: Grafana Labs, open source, monetization, plugin system, community engagement.
