When you ship code daily, performance degrades slowly—5 milliseconds here, 50 there—across dozens of releases. By the time everything feels slow, you've already lost your customers, and that's just the baseline. When an event like Black Friday hits, or when you go viral, small vulnerabilities become very expensive outages.

Grafana Cloud's performance and load testing, built on a powerful testing engine, helps your team stay ahead of all of this. It lets you continuously track performance trends, simulate traffic at scale, and ensure every release is ready for both everyday use and peak load of your applications.

With Grafana Cloud and our desktop application, developers can record real user flows right from the browser, even generate test scripts in seconds, and fine-tune them in a visual interface that makes it easy to build meaningful tests without deep scripting knowledge and domain expertise. Tests run across multiple regions come together in Grafana Cloud and are stored for in-depth analysis. This gives your organization one clear place to understand how performance is changing over time.

For every test, you can watch metrics in real time as they run. The performance overview shows response times and request rates, so you can confirm stability or spot bottlenecks early on in the process. For browser tests, you can review web metrics, timelines, and screenshots that show exactly how each page performs from your user's point of view.

The Grafana Assistant integrates directly with your tests, allowing your team to have it analyze results and surface insights that help explain why performance changes and where to focus your energy. 

From continuous testing to smarter insights, Grafana Cloud performance and load testing helps your team ensure every deployment performs the way it should at any scale. Get started with performance and load testing by signing up for Grafana Cloud for free today.
