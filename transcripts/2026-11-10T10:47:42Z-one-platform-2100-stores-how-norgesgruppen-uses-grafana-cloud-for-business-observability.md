## Description

Norway's largest grocery retailer runs 2,100 stores, 1 million order lines a day, and 8 million items sold — and they monitor all of it in Grafana Cloud.

NorgesGruppen's Karin (Director of Applications) and Torgeir (Tech Lead, Monitoring) share how they moved from receiving 12,000 unactionable alerts per month to running a fully proactive observability culture on Grafana Cloud.

## Summary

The show titled "One platform, 2,100 stores: How NorgesGruppen uses Grafana Cloud for business observability" was published on November 10, 2026. Speakers Karin, Director of Applications, and Torgeir, Tech Lead for Monitoring, discussed how NorgesGruppen, Norway's largest grocery retailer, transformed its observability practices using Grafana Cloud. They highlighted their journey from receiving 12,000 unactionable alerts per month to establishing a proactive observability culture, implementing tools like Grabot for incident automation, and Smart Checkout for computer vision monitoring. Key takeaways included the importance of cultural transformation in tech adoption and the effectiveness of Grafana in improving operational efficiency across their 2,100 stores.

Topics: Grafana Cloud, observability, incident automation, Smart Checkout, cultural transformation.
