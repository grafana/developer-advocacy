## Summary

The panel discussion titled "Lessons from the front lines of Observability with Nexi Group" took place on November 11, 2026, featuring Roman, Principal Architect at Nexi Group. The conversation centered around building an observability culture, leveraging AI for troubleshooting, and the importance of breaking down silos between development teams. Key takeaways included the value of AI agents in enhancing observability, the necessity of justifying observability costs to finance teams, and the cultural shift towards team-owned on-call responsibilities. Roman emphasized the importance of a unified observability platform and the proactive approach to incident management, suggesting that organizations should consider hiring seasoned Site Reliability Engineers (SREs) to guide their observability efforts.
