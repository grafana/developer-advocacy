## Summary

In the episode titled "Managing Collectors at Scale: OpAMP and Fleet Management" from the Grafana OTel Community Call #11, published on October 22, 2026, speakers Emma Valves (Developer Advocate at Grafana Labs), Marco, and Bijay discussed the Open Agent Management Protocol (OpAMP) and its role in managing OpenTelemetry collectors at scale. They highlighted how OpAMP addresses challenges such as configuration drift and collector health monitoring, emphasizing its importance for organizations managing thousands of collectors. Key takeaways included the significance of centralized management for collector configurations and the ongoing development of OpAMP as it moves towards stability. The speakers encouraged community involvement in contributing to the project and testing its features.
