# What it actually takes to make AI useful in observability — a fireside chat with thredd

Published on 2026-11-18T16:10:48Z

## Description

When thredd, an AI-first issuer processor, consolidated onto Grafana Cloud, it unlocked something unexpected: AI that could find root cause faster than the engineers themselves. But getting there required months of unglamorous work and aligning naming conventions, tagging every metric and log by team and service, and learning the hard way that good AI recommendations without full context can still cause production incidents. In this fireside chat, Teodor Baltaretu and Mark Swarbrick share what actually changed, what broke, and where they've drawn the line between AI and human judgment in payments.
