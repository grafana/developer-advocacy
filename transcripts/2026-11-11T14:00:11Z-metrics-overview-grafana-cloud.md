Published on 2026-11-11T14:00:11Z

## Description

Grafana Cloud Metrics helps teams monitor their systems and manage growing metric volume without losing the detail they need to understand what’s happening.

As applications and infrastructure grow, so do the metrics teams collect, query, and pay for. Grafana Cloud Metrics is a fully managed metrics service, so Grafana handles the infrastructure and maintenance while your team uses familiar dashboards, queries, and alerts to monitor services and troubleshoot issues.

You can explore metrics and investigate what’s happening with Grafana Assistant using natural language. Ask it questions about your metrics, then turn the results into visualizations, dashboards, and alerts. Adaptive Metrics also looks at how your team uses metrics across dashboards, alerts, recording rules, and queries. It recommends ways to aggregate underused metric data into lower-cardinality versions, helping reduce volume and costs while keeping the metrics your team relies on.

Built around open standards, Grafana Cloud Metrics supports Prometheus and OpenTelemetry, with Grafana Alloy and other compatible collectors to help keep your instrumentation portable and flexible.

If you're coming from a self-managed Prometheus or Mimir setup, you know that scaling and operating metrics backends can take a lot of work. If you're transitioning from another observability platform, the challenge may be keeping costs predictable as your team's metric volume grows. 

No matter where you're at in the observability journey, you need a metrics platform that can scale with your systems without adding more work for your team or forcing you to compromise on the visibility you need. That's what **Grafana Cloud Metrics** is built for. It provides your team with a fully managed metrics backend built on open standards like **Prometheus** and **OpenTelemetry**, along with tools to help you manage cost and cardinality as your metrics grow.

With Cloud Metrics, Grafana handles the infrastructure behind ingestion, query performance, and retention, allowing your team to focus on the questions your metrics can answer. You don't even need to be a **PromQL** expert to ask those questions anymore. With **Grafana Assistant**, you can explore and investigate your metrics using natural language.
