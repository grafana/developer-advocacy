## Summary

In the Big Tent podcast episode titled "An Anthropic Founder's Rule for When to Use an AI Agent," published on July 10, 2026, speakers discuss the innovative use of AI agents in complex systems. They emphasize the importance of creating adversarial relationships between AI components to enhance performance, drawing parallels to human organizational dynamics. The conversation highlights the effectiveness of employing multiple AI assistants to collaboratively tackle problems, with a "devil's advocate" role to challenge assumptions, mirroring human interactions. A key takeaway is that AI agents should be utilized when a task would typically require hiring and training a smart human.

Topics: AI agents, adversarial relationships, human organizations, collaborative problem-solving, training AI.

One of the most exciting things going on in these systems, as they become more complex, is the thoughtful implementation of adversarial relationships between two parts of the system. Here, one AI's purpose is to make another AI's work better in an iterative cycle. Having systems that are wired to work against each other in the spirit of a common goal is something that human organizations do really well at scale. This pattern offers a glimpse of how we can get this under control in some way before everything changes again.
