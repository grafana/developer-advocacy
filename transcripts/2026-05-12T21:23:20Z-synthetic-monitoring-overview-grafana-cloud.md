When something breaks in production, your users usually feel it before you do. With so many services, third-party dependencies, and apps you don't even fully control, it's hard to know what the real user experience actually looks like until it's way too late. 

**Grafana Cloud Synthetic Monitoring** was built to solve this exact problem. It enables your teams to proactively test the most important user journeys and APIs before your users ever touch them. You can start with simple uptime and availability checks or run full end-to-end journeys like logging in, searching, or checking out. 

Synthetic monitoring mimics the exact path your users take, giving you signals that internal metrics or logs oftentimes seem to miss. You can manage tests visually or entirely as code, making it easy to version, review, and integrate into your existing development workflows. Your tests can run from Grafana Cloud's global locations to see how customers experience your app around the world or from inside your own network. 

When you need to monitor internal systems, securely all the results flow straight into Grafana Cloud, giving your team real-time visibility into uptime, latency, errors, and performance trends. This provides a clear, reliable picture of how your app is performing from the user's perspective. 

With synthetic monitoring, you can validate that everything works the way your users actually expect it to and catch problems before they impact your business. So get started with **Grafana Cloud Synthetic Monitoring** for free today.
