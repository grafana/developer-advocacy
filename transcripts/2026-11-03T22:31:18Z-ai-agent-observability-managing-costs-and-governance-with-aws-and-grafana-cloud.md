## Summary

The show is titled "AI Agent Observability: Managing Costs and Governance with AWS and Grafana Cloud," published on November 3, 2026. The speaker, who leads the AI go-to-market strategy across AWS, discussed the challenges of AI governance and observability, particularly in relation to managing costs associated with AI agents. Key topics included the increasing costs of AI agents, the importance of observability for compliance with regulations like the EU AI Act, and how Grafana Cloud and AWS can help organizations gain visibility and control over their AI expenditures. The discussion emphasized the need for open standards and the integration of tools like OpenTelemetry to enhance observability across various data sources.

Topics: AI governance, observability, cost management, Grafana Cloud, AWS, OpenTelemetry, EU AI Act.
