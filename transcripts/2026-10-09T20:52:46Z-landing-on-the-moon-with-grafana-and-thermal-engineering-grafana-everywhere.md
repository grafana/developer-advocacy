My name is Jackson Sweeney, and I'm a thermal engineer at Firefly Aerospace. On March 2nd, 2025, Firefly Aerospace became the first commercial company to complete a fully successful moon landing, and Grafana helped us do it. I was responsible for maintaining the temperatures of the Blue Ghost lunar lander. 

Space can be a very relentless thermal environment, and it is my job to ensure that all of the electronics and various components on board stay at a happy temperature. Especially on the moon, temperatures can drop below -100 degrees Celsius and rise above +100 degrees Celsius. My role is to keep the entire vehicle healthy and happy at those extreme temperatures.

When controlling a spacecraft, we need to be able to make quick decisions because one wrong decision could compromise the entire mission. Something that was very important to me was the direction that the vehicle was pointed in. If the sun is hitting one side of the vehicle, that side will get hot while the other side will get cold because it is not receiving any sunlight. I used the dashboard to monitor the direction of the sun and how it affected the temperatures of the vehicle to choose how we oriented the spacecraft in space.

Something that's very special to me is that there was a plaque with all of our names engraved on it. For me to be able to put my family's name on the moon is something that is pretty emotional. 

Winning the Golden Grot this year is simply super exciting. This is a super smart community of engineers and overall great people. It's not something that I really had on my bingo card for this year, and when I was presented with the opportunity to apply, I knew I had to do it. I am super grateful, super excited to be here, and super glad that it is happening.
