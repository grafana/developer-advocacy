# How the LGTM Stack changed the observability culture at Wise Payments | ObservabilityCON 2023

Published on 2023-11-17T17:52:25Z

## Description

The observability team at Wise Payments – Europe’s leader in cross-border money transfers – had long provided the company’s developers access to a multitude of tools. 

But as costs and complexity increased, Ibukun Itimi, Engineering Lead for Observability and Andrew Brown, Reliability Squad Lead, saw an opportunity to change not only the tools they were using, but also the observability culture. 

In this session, Ibukun and Andrew highlight how the Grafana LGTM Stack empowered their team to become more product-focused and shift from being external tool wranglers to providing in-house observability expertise.

## Summary

In the talk titled "How the LGTM Stack changed the observability culture at Wise Payments," presented at ObservabilityCON 2023 on November 17, 2023, speakers Ibukun Itimi, Engineering Lead for Observability, and Andrew Brown, Reliability Squad Lead, discussed the evolution of observability at Wise Payments. They highlighted the transition from a complex array of monitoring tools to a more streamlined observability culture using the Grafana LGTM Stack, which includes Grafana Mimir, Tempo, and Loki. Key takeaways included the importance of scalability, user experience, and measuring the value of observability tools to enhance the engineering team's efficiency. The speakers emphasized the need for a unified observability platform that simplifies data access and improves incident response times, while also considering the balance between building in-house solutions and leveraging vendor partnerships.

Topics: observability, Grafana LGTM Stack, Wise Payments, scalability, user experience
