## Summary

In the episode titled "Build apps on your data with Grafana Pages" from The Context Window on November 5, 2026, hosts Nicole VanderHoeven, Tiffany, Stephanie, and Tom discussed the new Grafana Pages feature introduced at Observability Con. Grafana Pages allows users to create live interactive data applications directly from their Grafana instance with minimal coding, leveraging AI to generate visualizations and dashboards. Key takeaways included the ease of creating engaging data presentations, the integration of various data sources, and the potential for personal and organizational use cases. The discussion highlighted the importance of user feedback for future enhancements and the current limitations, such as sharing options and multi-user editing capabilities.

Topics: Grafana Pages, Observability Con, interactive data applications, AI integration, user feedback.
