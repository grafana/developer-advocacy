## Summary

In the video titled "When to Use Grafana Assistant vs. MCP vs. GCX: Part 1," published on August 17, 2026, Nicole discusses the distinctions between Grafana Assistant, Grafana MCP, and GCX, emphasizing that they are complementary rather than competitive. Grafana Assistant serves as a comprehensive reasoning layer that manages contacts, dashboards, and data sources, making it ideal for visual or exploratory tasks, such as building dashboards or conducting root cause analyses. The key takeaway is that while Assistant acts as the "brain" of Grafana, the MCP and GCX serve as the "hands" that can also drive Grafana's functionalities.

Grafana has three AI offerings that people often confuse: Assistant, Grafana MCP, and GCX. In this discussion, I will explain each one and help you determine which is best for your use case. Spoiler alert: it may not be just one. These tools are not competitors; you can think of them as a brain and two hands.

Let's start with the brain, which is Assistant. Assistant is more than just a chatbot; it is a comprehensive reasoning layer. It manages your contacts and understands your Grafana setup, including your dashboards, data sources, and the knowledge graph of your infrastructure. Instead of just clicking around to accomplish tasks, you can simply tell Assistant what you want to do, and it will figure out how to do it.

Reach for Assistant when your work is visual or exploratory. For example, if you want to build a dashboard, ask Assistant. If you need guidance on how to do something or where to find a specific feature in the UI, Assistant can help. If you encounter a real-life incident and need a root cause analysis, Assistant is the tool to use. In fact, that is its sweet spot. Assistant can form a hypothesis, correlate across signals, and even open a pull request with the fix.

Here's the key point: Assistant is Grafana's own brain, but it is not the only brain that can drive Grafana. That's where the two hands come in, which I will discuss next.
