## Summary

The show is titled "Observability for a Privacy-first AI Wearable | Grafana Everywhere," published on June 18, 2026. The speaker is Dhananjay Yadav, the CEO and co-founder of NeoSapien, who discusses the development of AI wearables designed to prioritize user privacy by eliminating audio storage. He highlights the use of Grafana Assistant to optimize their workflow, quickly identifying and resolving bottlenecks in their pipeline, particularly in BLE firmware, which significantly enhances user experience. Yadav also expresses pride in winning the Golden Grot Award, recognizing their product as a leading observability system in the AI space.
