Did you know you can now use Grafana's AI assistant anywhere you work? Yes, even in your own self-hosted Grafana instance. Let me show you how easy it is to get set up.

First, jump on over to your local Grafana instance. In the navigation menu above, click on **Administration** and then select **Plugins**.
