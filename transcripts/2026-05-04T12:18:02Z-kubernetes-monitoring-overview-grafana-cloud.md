Monitoring Kubernetes at scale is hard because your environment is constantly changing. Workloads are shifting, nodes are coming and going, and services are created and destroyed all the time. Whether your team is running a few clusters in a handful of regions or hundreds of clusters across the globe, the challenge is the same: staying on top of what's actually happening.

Today, that often means switching cluster contexts and running commands in the terminal just to understand basic health and utilization. That doesn't scale, and your team needs better visibility without more operational overhead. **Grafana Cloud's Kubernetes monitoring** is built to give your team exactly that clarity. It provides a centralized view across all of your clusters with key insights like CPU and memory usage, along with out-of-the-box alerts that surface issues as they emerge.

From there, your team can drill into any cluster or resource to quickly understand usage from both a performance and cost perspective. With the **Grafana Assistant**, your team can move beyond surface-level metrics and run deeper analysis without manually digging through dashboards. When you click into an individual resource, everything your team needs is in one single unified view of metrics, logs, and events, along with focused views for CPU, memory, storage, networking, and more.

The Grafana Assistant is embedded directly into Kubernetes monitoring, helping highlight potential issues, explaining what's affected, and guiding your team towards next steps. Getting started is extremely straightforward. Your team chooses the features you want, generates a Helm chart, and deploys in minutes on any Kubernetes cluster anywhere you run.

So go get started with **Grafana Cloud** for free today and give your team a faster way to understand Kubernetes at scale.
