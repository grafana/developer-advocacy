# Meet Assistant Workspace: Full-page experience for Grafana Assistant

Published on 2026-07-27T15:30:33Z

## Description

Introducing Assistant Workspace, a full-page surface for Grafana Assistant, built for the conversations that outgrow a sidebar.

URL: https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=3eh2DPeO1nw

## Summary

The show "Meet Assistant Workspace: Full-page experience for Grafana Assistant" was published on July 27, 2026. Ed, a product designer at Grafana, introduced the new Assistant Workspace, a dedicated full-page interface for Grafana Assistant that enhances user interaction beyond the sidebar. Key features include a conversation history area, a main chat section, and a canvas for visualizing charts and data, allowing for a more immersive experience. The workspace also facilitates better navigation through conversations and offers options to share discussions or save them as dashboards. Ed expressed excitement about the potential of the workspace and encouraged community feedback for future improvements.

Topics: Grafana Assistant, Assistant Workspace, user experience, data visualization, community feedback.

Hi, I'm Ed. I'm a product designer here at Grafana, and today I want to talk to you about our new way of interacting with Assistant: Workspace. 

Assistant has lived in the sidebar for the past year, and we think it's a fantastic addition to Grafana. It really helps you in your day-to-day whenever you are navigating through Grafana and doing your tasks there. However, we wanted to answer the question: what does a chat-first or agent-first experience in Grafana look like? To address that, we built Workspace.

Inside your sidebar, you will now see a new button at the top called **Open in Workspace**. By clicking this, you are taken to a dedicated UI, which is comprised of three main areas: 

1. Your conversation history on the left-hand side
2. Your main chat
3. An area that we call **Canvas**, which we are really excited about.

The funny thing about demoing AI tools is the bit in the middle where everyone cuts out and pretends that it goes really, really quickly in there. Just hard cut.

As normal conversations unfold, we've slightly changed the format in how you interact with them. Instead of everything being stuck into a single column like you have in the sidebar, inside Canvas, we now bring the charts out and other UI elements to give you a much richer, more in-depth experience. You can now bring these charts full screen and really dive into your data at a much more detailed level.

Similarly, when you are running long conversations, it can be quite difficult to figure out exactly where you are or what has happened in the conversation, particularly when you are running multiple at once. Canvas really helps you with this. When you switch back to an old conversation, instead of having to scroll up through the UI to figure out the various parts and where the various bits of data happened, we now bring these out into a dedicated experience that allows you to immediately see the important information at a glance.

At the bottom of this conversation, you can see a new card that we've created called **Start Investigation**. Instead of running investigations, we'll continue the conversation here. As the conversation has unfolded, more and more bits of information have been added to our sources tab. This has brought a lot of the key highlights out of our chat and made them easily accessible. It even includes things like mermaid diagrams showing how everything is connected together.

If you want to bring this outside of Workspace and involve more people, you have a couple of options. You can share the conversation and bring people in. If you want this as an artifact, we have the ability to **Save as Dashboard**, and clicking that will take you into the normal dashboard edit flow. 

Sometimes, it's also nice to understand the data behind the charts and ensure that the LLM is generating everything you want it to. We have the ability to open these visualizations and this data inside of **Explore**, which is particularly useful if you want to understand the underlying queries and how these charts are built.

Finally, you have the ability to filter conversations in the sidebar to really drill down to the stuff that is most relevant to you. We'll be demoing investigations later on in the week. Here, you can easily jump between conversations that you've created with Assistant, and everything inside of here that you've had conversations with in the sidebar will be present in this interface. 

No matter where you interact with Assistant—be it via mobile, in your browser, or in Workspace itself—you can guarantee that all your conversations will appear in one place. 

We're really excited about Workspace and all the different things we'll be able to do with it. We'd love to hear more from you about what you would like to see inside of this interface. We're excited to tease investigations, which is our first example of something really exciting to use inside of Workspace. The possibilities are really endless here, and we'd love to hear more ideas from the community. 

If you have access to Assistant, you will also have access to Workspace. So, check it out now!
