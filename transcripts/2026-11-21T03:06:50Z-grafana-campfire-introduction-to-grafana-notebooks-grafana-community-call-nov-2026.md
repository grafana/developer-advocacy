## Description

Join us for an introduction to Grafana Notebooks, a new feature that brings your notes, queries, visualizations, and Grafana Assistant findings together in a single, shareable working document.

No more investigations scattered across tabs, screenshots, and chat threads. We’ll walk through how to create a notebook, then look at how you and your team can use them to explore a question about your data, document an investigation so the next person doesn’t have to start from scratch, and prepare post-incident reviews with the right panels already in place.

We’ll also show you how to find, search, filter, edit, and export your notebooks from the new Notebooks page, and how to enable Notebooks in your own Grafana instance (currently in public preview).

If you are new to Notebooks, or just curious? This is the perfect opportunity to learn the basics, bring your questions, and share your feedback with us.

*Hosts:* Syed Usman Ahmad 
*Experts:* Daniel Moosdeen, Juan Cabanas and Haris Rozajac

*BONUS Content (If we get some extra time)* We will give a quick demo of Cross-dashboard variables, let you define a variable once, at global or folder scope, and reuse it across many dashboards, so names and definitions stay consistent without copy-pasting. You can manage them as code alongside your dashboards, so changes are versioned and reviewable.
