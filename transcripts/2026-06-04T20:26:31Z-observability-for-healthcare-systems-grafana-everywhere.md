## Summary

The community call titled "Observability for Healthcare Systems | Grafana Everywhere" took place on June 4, 2026, featuring Oren Lion, who leads logistics engineering at TeleTracking. Oren discussed how TeleTracking utilizes Grafana to optimize patient flow in hospitals, emphasizing the importance of incident response and the role of Grafana Assistant in reducing the lifecycle of incidents. Key takeaways included the significance of democratizing metrics and the integration of multiple data sources into Grafana to enhance observability and streamline incident management.

My name is Oren Lion. I lead logistics engineering at TeleTracking. At TeleTracking, we aim to optimize the flow of patients through hospitals. 

Let's start with what happens when that doesn't work out: ambulances get turned around and surgeries get delayed. Our platform enables patient care to run smoothly. We use Grafana to ensure our availability and performance. 

One thing I really want to underscore in our team's winning of the Grott award is that we're looking at the entire life cycle of a problem, like incident response, and how we can reduce that life cycle. It has many steps, and if you use Grafana Assistant in each step, we start to see the whole life cycle get reduced. 

For example, when an incident is raised, if we start using Grafana Assistant early and often, we see that we can reduce the incident response root cause analysis phase. This leads to us reducing the time it takes to do post-incident reporting because Grafana Assistant has already baked in parts of the post-incident process. 

Democratizing metrics is a theme within Grafana. When I think about that, I reflect on our origin story, where we had multiple data sources all accessible through Grafana. In our environment, we have multiple data sources that, until they get to Grafana, are inaccessible. 

Once we have all signals leading to Grafana, we can use Grafana Assistant to shape the data and tell us why things are happening. That's where we're actually seeing democratizing observability.
