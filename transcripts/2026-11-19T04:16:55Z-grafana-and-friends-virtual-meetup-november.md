## Description

In Grafana and Friends Virtual Meetup - November, the first online edition of the community meetup, three community speakers share what they've built and learned with Grafana. Carike covers how to design inclusive, accessible dashboards that people actually use. Emmanuel builds Fantasy Premier League dashboards from a JSON API with the Infinity data source and Grafana Assistant. Rashi shows how to give an AI agent access to Grafana through MCP without handing over your API token, using Docker Sandboxes.

Can't make it to a local Grafana & Friends meetup, or don't have one nearby? Join us online! Grafana & Friends Virtual brings the community together, wherever you are. All talks come from community members sharing their projects, experiences and lessons learnt, at every level from beginner to Grafana veteran.
