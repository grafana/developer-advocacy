## Summary

The show is titled "Incident Response Management (IRM) overview | Grafana Cloud," published on May 12, 2026. The discussion focuses on Grafana Cloud's Incident Response Management (IRM) solution, which aims to streamline on-call management, alert routing, and incident resolution by consolidating various tools into a single platform. Key features include integration with tools like Slack and Jira, on-call schedule management, and the use of Assistant Investigations to provide real-time insights and recommendations during incidents. The main takeaway is that Grafana IRM enhances team efficiency and incident resolution speed, ultimately improving organizational performance.

You can have incredible observability coverage, great dashboards, and even alerting for all your services. But oftentimes, these are spread across a bunch of different tools, and that means your team's efficiency starts to fall. All the context switching your team does across their tools on a daily basis creates a poor experience. So when an incident does happen, it takes longer and longer to identify. This means your customers start to feel that impact. Maybe you, as an engineering team, feel that impact, and worst of all, the entire organization can feel that impact.

Now imagine your incident response living right next to your telemetry, with all your data, all your alerts, all in one place. That's why Grafana Cloud's Incident Response Management (IRM) was built—to give your team a faster, unified view into resolving incidents all in one platform. You can connect Grafana Cloud's IRM to the tools you're already deeply integrated with, like Slack, Microsoft Teams, and even Jira. 

From there, it's quick to set up your on-call schedules directly in Grafana Cloud and then sync them with your calendar, so engineers on call get the right alerts at the right time, and most importantly, in the right channel. Once setup is complete, Grafana IRM starts to close the loop from receiving an alert to actually troubleshooting the incident with Assistant Investigations. The Assistant automatically works behind the scenes, begins scanning your telemetry, correlating it all together, and giving you hypotheses and recommendations on how to solve within minutes. 

This means your team can discover the root cause quickly and get back to focusing on innovation as your teams work through the incident. IRM captures the full story for you, including timelines, relevant Grafana visualizations, and key messages across your teams, all organized in a single place. This means that when the dust settles and services are back to healthy, Grafana IRM helps you streamline your retrospective with performance insights over time. Each incident makes your team better and actually improves your operations. 

So give your team a unified experience and get started with Grafana Cloud IRM for free today.
