## Summary

The show is titled "Grafana Customer Testimonial - Philip Pencal Alter Domus," published on June 22, 2026. In this testimonial, Philip Benko, Head of Site Reliability Engineering at OutSystems, discusses the company's journey to centralize their observability platform. After evaluating 22 suppliers, they chose Grafana, resulting in a significant 55% reduction in costs without sacrificing observability quality. He highlights the importance of Grafana in understanding application performance and optimizing operations, particularly through the use of observability AI introduced by Verifone.

Topics: observability, Grafana, cost reduction, observability AI, application performance.

My name is Philip Benko. I'm the head of site reliability engineering at OutSystems. Since I assumed the leadership for observability at OutSystems, one of the major challenges has been to ensure we centralize all the observability in one single platform. We have a lot of segregations and initiatives, but none of these initiatives were actually moving towards being our platform—something we can trust. This makes all the difference for us because our customers expect the same trust on our side. We need to trust our observability platform as well.

So, we started to evaluate different tools in the market, and we ended up doing a very strict process for consolidating our observability. Our procurement process took around six months to complete, during which we evaluated 22 suppliers. In the second phase, we narrowed it down to four suppliers until we ultimately chose Grafana as our core platform for observability.

The moment I completed the migration and checked the results, it was a shock because we reduced our bill by 55%. I went to the board of directors and presented, "Hey, with this migration, we cut 55% of the cost." There was silence in the room. Everybody was thinking, "Okay, what is the trick? What do we lose after we did this migration?" The answer was nothing. We still maintained the same levels, standards, and quality of observability that we had before, but at half the price. The board was silent; they didn't believe it until I actually showed the numbers in Grafana and demonstrated that we covered all the observability with all the resources.

Our agentic applications are totally different in terms of what you can predict. All the pumps can define different strategies and routes, making it quite impossible to understand everything 100% without looking at traces, metrics, and observability overall. So, I reached out to Verifone and asked, "Hey, we need a solution for this case. You're able to collect metrics and information about your applications, but everything that goes after the LLM takes decisions is a black box for us."

Verifone introduced the observability AI. We implemented it on the same day, and after a few days of using it in production, the difference was amazing. We started to collect information about our MCP servers, the prompts, and the conversations. This allowed us to understand why our MCP servers were failing and why our users were not able to achieve what they were looking for when they tried to reach out to these MCP servers. 

We also used it for cost control with tokenization and to optimize our routines for the agents, as we were able to understand what the users were asking through the conversations. For the teams that are using agents on a daily basis as part of the products, if you're concerned about cost control and optimizing your solution, you definitely should check out the observability AI.
