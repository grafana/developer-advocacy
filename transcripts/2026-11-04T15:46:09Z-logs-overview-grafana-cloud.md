# Logs overview | Grafana Cloud

Published on 2026-11-04T15:46:09Z

## Description

Grafana Cloud Logs helps teams manage growing log volumes without giving up the visibility they need when something breaks.

As applications and services grow, so do the logs teams need to ingest, store, query, and pay for. Grafana Cloud Logs is a fully managed logging platform, so Grafana handles the infrastructure, patching, and maintenance while your team focuses on finding answers in its log data.

You can explore and investigate logs with Grafana Assistant using natural language, without needing to know LogQL. Ask it to find errors in critical services, then turn the results into visualizations, dashboards, and alerts. Adaptive Logs also analyzes how your team uses its log data and recommends high-volume logs you may be able to reduce before ingestion, helping you make cost decisions based on actual usage.

Built around open standards, Grafana Cloud Logs supports OpenTelemetry with Grafana Alloy and other compatible collectors, helping keep your instrumentation portable and flexible.

Now, whether you're running your logging platform yourself or using another observability solution, log management eventually catches up with you as your company grows. More applications mean more logs, and more logs mean more to ingest, store, query, and even pay for. But when something breaks—and it always does—logs are still one of the first places your team goes to look. So the answer can't be to simply collect less. You need to keep the visibility while also making the backend easier to operate and the cost easier to manage.

That's where **Grafana Cloud Logs** comes in. It's a fully managed logging platform designed to scale with you and your team. Grafana takes care of all the infrastructure, the patching, and the maintenance behind the platform, so your team can just focus on the questions your logs can answer for you. You don't even need to be a LogQL expert to ask those questions anymore. 

With the **Grafana Assistant**, you can explore and investigate your logs using natural language. Just pop open the assistant window in the top right corner and ask it to find error logs in your most critical services. The assistant will respond with breakdowns and visuals, which can be transformed into dashboards and alerts directly from that window.

**Cloud Logs** also gives you a way to get ahead of rising costs. With **Adaptive Logs**, you can look at how your team is using the log data, and it even recommends high-volume logs you may be able to reduce before they're ingested. That means you can make those decisions based on the actual usage of your team instead of guessing just what to keep.

Because **Cloud Logs** is built around open standards, you can collect logs with **OpenTelemetry** using **Grafana Alloy** or another compatible collector, keeping your data and instrumentation portable and flexible. 

So with **Grafana Cloud Logs**, you get a logging platform that scales with your systems, helps you find answers without having to learn a new query language, and even gives you more control over cost without having to operate all that backend yourself. So go get started with **Grafana Cloud** for free today.
