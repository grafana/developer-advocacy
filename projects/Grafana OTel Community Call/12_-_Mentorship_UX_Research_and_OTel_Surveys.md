**Title:** From Mentorship to Better OTel Developer Experience: UX Research and Surveys (Grafana ❤️‍🔥 OpenTelemetry Community Call #12) **[VERIFY title with guests]**

**Description:**

In this episode of the Grafana OTel Community Call, we're joined by Victoria Nduka and Andrej Kiripolsky from Grafana Labs to talk about what happens when UX research meets open source. Victoria completed an LFX mentorship doing UX research on how Prometheus should handle OpenTelemetry resource attributes, with Andrej as one of the mentors, and kept contributing to the OpenTelemetry End User SIG long after it ended.

We'll look at what the research found, what the mentorship made possible, and how the End User SIG uses surveys to hear from the people using OpenTelemetry. Andrej will also tell us what has been going on with the OTel surveys, and what is coming next.

- The research: Prometheus and OTel resource attributes
  - The problem in plain words: resource attributes become labels, and cardinality can explode
  - Three approaches today: map everything to labels, selectively promote, or `target_info` with joins
  - Method: 6 stakeholder interviews, 7 user interviews, a survey with 134 responses (61 using OTel and Prometheus together)
  - Findings that surprised you: users don't distinguish resource attributes from labels, join syntax is a barrier, 78% cite documentation gaps, many don't know the "telescoping" pattern
  - What has changed since the report? Docs updates, the UX Research Working Group introduced at PromCon EU 2025 **[VERIFY what has landed]**
  - Longer term ideas: entities, first-class metadata storage in Prometheus
- Surveys as a DevEx tool for OTel (Andrej)
  - What the End User SIG does and why surveys matter for maintainers
  - Prometheus and OTel interoperability survey 2026 (published Sept 2026): 186 responses, 81 analysed, ease of use up from 3.1 to 3.6, "hard to use together" down from 29% to 10% **[VERIFY numbers are final]**
  - What the open-ended answers asked for: unified data models, better resource attribute and metadata handling, less naming friction. What happened at the Prometheus Dev Summit in October? **[VERIFY]**
  - How a survey goes from an idea to a blog post: questions, template, privacy, data in the `sig-end-user` repo, analysis, presenting to maintainers
  - Past surveys: Docs Usability (2024), Developer Experience and Contributor Experience (2025), Collector follow-up and Japanese community survey (2026)
- The new survey **[VERIFY which one and what can be said before launch]**
